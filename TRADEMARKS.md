- Truthful references to the Lerro project.
- Links to the official repository or release pages.
- Statements that software is compatible with, based on, or forked from

- Unmodified screenshots used for reporting, education, or commentary with

- Naming a modified build, fork, service, company, domain, or package “Lerro”.
- Using the Lerro logo or app icon as the primary identity of a modified build.

  suggests endorsement.

Public forks should choose their own product name and app icon. They may retain
accurate attribution in documentation and About surfaces.

Documentation, screenshots, and templates may carry a CC BY 4.0 license while
also displaying a Lerro mark. The trademark terms in this file continue to
govern use of the mark itself.

Questions can be opened as a repository discussion without including
confidential information. This policy is project guidance and does not limit
