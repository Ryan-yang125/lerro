# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The Lerro community is a place for focused, respectful collaboration. Everyone
participating in repositories, discussions, issues, reviews, events, and other
project spaces is expected to:

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith while evaluating evidence.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about work and ideas.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and access
  needs.
- Protect personal data, confidential reports, and contributor safety.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and help keep technical discussions useful.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, restrict
participation, or ban an account when necessary to protect the community. They
will consider context, impact, repetition, prior guidance, and participant
safety.

For routine concerns, flag the content for maintainers and explain the impact.
For immediate safety or platform-abuse concerns, use GitHub's private reporting
and abuse channels. Ask for a private maintainer channel without posting
sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers handling a report should share it only with people needed to assess
and resolve the concern, preserve relevant evidence, avoid conflicts of
interest, and communicate the outcome as privacy allows.

This policy applies whenever someone represents the Lerro project or
participates in a project-managed space.
